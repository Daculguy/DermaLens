# DermaLens Terms Of Use Draft

Draft updated June 12, 2026. This is product/legal drafting material, not legal advice. Review with counsel before public release. If no custom EULA is configured in App Store Connect, Apple's standard EULA may also apply.

## Cosmetic Guidance Only

DermaLens provides cosmetic skincare guidance, routine organization, product comparison, and progress tracking. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, prevent disease, prescribe medication, or replace advice from a licensed clinician.

## When To Seek Care

See a clinician promptly for painful, changing, bleeding, suspicious, infected, rapidly worsening, or urgent skin concerns. Do not delay professional care because of an app result.

## Subscriptions

DermaLens Premium is sold through Apple in-app purchase as auto-renewable monthly and yearly subscriptions. Premium unlocks deeper ingredient reasoning, saved report history, progress comparisons, product alternatives, and expanded shelf tracking.

Payment is charged to the user's Apple account after purchase confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled in Apple account settings before renewal. Apple manages billing, cancellation, refunds, and payment method details.

## Product Recommendations

Recommendations depend on scan quality, user-entered preferences, available product data, ingredient-list coverage, evidence level, and disclosed commercial relationships. Product labels can change; users should verify labels, avoid known irritants, and patch test when appropriate.

## Commercial Relationships

Any affiliate, brand partner, owned-brand, sponsored, or otherwise paid relationship must be disclosed in product metadata and ranking explanations before public launch. Commercial relationships should lower trust/ranking confidence rather than appear equivalent to independent verified products.

## Acceptable Use

Use DermaLens only for personal skincare planning. Do not use it for emergency care, medical diagnosis, clinical screening, prescription decisions, or decisions about suspicious lesions.
